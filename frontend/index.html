How this works
Your rule is a regular expression. It gets deployed in front of the target, and every incoming request is checked against it. If it matches, the request is blocked with a 403.

We then replay your winning attack against the patched target. If the flag still comes back, your rule was bypassed.

Regex basics
. means "any character" — to match a literal dot, escape it: \.
\.\./ matches the three characters ../
| means "or" — (\.\.|%2e%2e) matches either form
/etc/ matches that text anywhere in the request
